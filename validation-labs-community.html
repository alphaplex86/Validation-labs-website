Validation Labs —
Private Slack Community

For operators turning uncertainty into decisions.

This is a private Slack community for operators working in early-stage validation and venture studio environments. Founders, product leaders, growth operators, analysts, and studio builders.

People who are actively testing ideas, markets, and business models. If you’re working through uncertainty with evidence, iteration, and clear decisions, you’ll fit in.

What This Community Is

A focused space for people doing validation work.

The Validation Labs community exists to:

  • Compare real validation work in progress
  • Pressure-test assumptions before capital is committed
  • Share experiments, results, and learnings
  • Discuss problem discovery, solution validation, GTM fit, and moats

Who This Is For

Founders & Operators

Building early-stage startups.

Studio Builders

Working in or around venture studios.

Functional Leaders

Product, growth, research, or operations.

Active Validators

Actively testing problems & solutions.

Participation Expectations

This is a working community. Participation keeps it valuable. Members are expected to:

  • Contribute past and present experience and insight.
  • Respectfully challenge assumptions and share relevant evidence.
  • Keep discussions practical, respectful, and focused on progress.

What You’ll Get Access To

  • Curated Slack channels focused on key stages of validation
  • Ongoing discussions tied to real ventures and active experiments
  • Early access to Validation Labs thinking, frameworks, and tools
  • Opportunities to collaborate, contribute ideas, or support research
